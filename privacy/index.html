Privacy

Privacy policy.

This policy explains what personal data anowarhdesign.com collects, why, who receives it and how to have it deleted. In short: the site records usage statistics, and if you send the contact form, your message reaches my inbox so I can reply. Nothing is sold and there is no advertising.

Last updated 20 November 2026

Who is responsible

This website is run by Anowar Hossain, an independent designer and developer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. For any privacy question or request, write to hello@anowarhdesign.com.

What is collected

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, WhatsApp number if you give one, budget range, project details and the language of the page you wrote from. It is sent to my inbox by email through Resend.
  • Usage statistics. Google Analytics records the pages viewed, the referring site, device and browser type and an approximate location derived from your IP address, and sets cookies to tell visits apart. Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights count page views and loading speed without cookies.
  • Technical data. Like every website, the hosting provider (Vercel) processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages. The contact form holds your IP address in memory for up to 15 minutes to block abuse; it is not stored.
  • Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers.
  • Browser storage. Your language choice and, in the desktop demo on the homepage, your wallpaper are saved in your own browser. They are not sent to me.
  • Booking and project forms. The booking link opens Cal.com and the project form opens Tally. What you enter there is handled under their privacy policies and passed on to me.

What it is used for

Enquiries are used to reply to you, prepare a quote and run the project if we work together. Statistics are used to see which pages are read and to fix slow or broken ones. I do not sell personal data, show advertising or build profiles of visitors.

Who receives it

Only the services needed to run the site: Vercel (hosting), Google (Analytics and Fonts), Resend (email delivery), Cal.com (bookings) and Tally (project form). Some of them process data in the United States or in other countries outside your own.

How long it is kept

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as the conversation or project lasts and afterwards for my business records, until you ask me to delete them. Usage statistics are kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics and Vercel and are then deleted automatically.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, corrected or sent to you, email hello@anowarhdesign.com from the address you used and say what you want done. I act on requests within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Security

The site is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), enquiries go to a mailbox only I use, and the providers above apply their own security measures. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send passwords or payment details through the form.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on; the site works the same without them. If you live in the EU, the UK or another region with data protection law, you also have the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect their data.

Android apps

Apps I publish on Google Play under the developer name Anowar Hossain each have their own privacy policy, which names the app and lists exactly what it collects. It is linked from the app’s Google Play listing and inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. If the translated versions of this page differ from the English one, the English version applies.
